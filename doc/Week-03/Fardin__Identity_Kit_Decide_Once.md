## 🔤 1. Typography Choices: One Family, Strict Hierarchy

| Level / Role | Font Family | Weight & Size | Design Rule & Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Page Titles (H1)** | **Geist** *(Google Fonts)* | Bold (700), 32px / 2.0rem | Tight tracking (`-0.03em`). Maximum punch; states claims plainly without decoration. |
| **Section Heads (H2)** | **Geist** | Semi-bold (600), 20px / 1.25rem | Clear division of case beats; calm authority without visual clutter. |
| **Body & Narrative** | **Geist** | Regular (400), 16px / 1.0rem | Generous line-height (1.65). Ultra-high legibility for technical explanations. |
| **Code & Telemetry** | **JetBrains Mono** | Regular (400), 13.5px / 0.85rem | Used for DuckDB queries, schema types, and quantitative metric tokens. |

## ☀️ 3. Accessibility & Sunlight Readability Audit

* **Mobile Sunlight Check:** The primary text color (`#09090B`) against the soft background (`#FAFAFA`) provides a massive 19.3:1 contrast ratio, ensuring text remains crisp and readable even under direct outdoor sunlight on mobile displays.
* **Accent Discipline:** The single accent color (`#0284C7`) is strictly reserved for interactive CTAs, active links, and metric badges. It never bleeds into text blocks or backgrounds, ensuring that real screenshots and benchmark charts are the only colorful objects on the screen.
